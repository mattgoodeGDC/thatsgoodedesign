Know Thyself to Lead Others

Team workshop

Know how you lead before you lead others.

A three-hour workshop built on every participant’s CliftonStrengths report. Your team learns how each person does their best work and what they need from colleagues, and leaves with an Owner’s Manual template, the first step toward the full workshop.

Book thirty minutes For companies or for schools

What is in it

  • Built on CliftonStrengthsEvery participant takes CliftonStrengths, Top 5 or the report for Managers or Leaders, so the room starts from data rather than introductions. No OBD needed.
  • Over half the time hands-onThe room works with its own results, not a lecture about someone else’s.
  • What is in the roomAll 34 strengths posters, a cheat sheet and an Owner’s Manual template.
  • From 5 to 50 peopleThree hours, virtual or in person, for a whole faculty, a department or a leadership group.

How it runs

  1. Before the workshop

    Take CliftonStrengths

    Every participant completes the assessment, and their report is ordered for them.

  2. In the room

    Read it together

    The team works through its results: how each person does their best work and what they need.

  3. What comes next

    The Owner’s Manual

    The natural next step: a separate workshop where each person writes the manual and the team swaps.
