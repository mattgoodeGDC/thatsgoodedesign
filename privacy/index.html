Legal

Privacy, and what this site does not do.

This site sets no cookies, runs no ad networks, and does no cross-site tracking. If you never type anything into it, it never learns who you are.

What follows is the detail, including the places where a third party is involved and your data leaves this site.

Measurement

How visits are counted

Visits are measured with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It is cookieless, it stores nothing on your device, and it reports aggregate figures such as page views and referrers rather than anything identifying you. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, the script does not load at all.

Hosting

Where the site lives

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, its servers process the ordinary request information needed to deliver a page, including your IP address. That is GitHub’s processing, under GitHub’s own privacy terms, and Goode Design & Consulting does not receive or retain those logs.

Typefaces are served by Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google and Google receives your IP address in the process.

If you get in touch

What happens to what you send

The mailing list

Email signups, including the free chapter, are handled by Kit (formerly ConvertKit), which stores your address and sends the mail. You can unsubscribe from any message, and unsubscribing removes you.

Booking a call

Scheduling runs through Calendly. What you enter there, typically a name, an email and whatever you write in the notes, is held by Calendly and visible to Matt.

Email to Matt

Mail sent directly is kept as long as it is useful and is not added to any list without you asking.

Never

What is not done with any of it

Your information is not sold, rented or traded. It is not given to advertising networks or data brokers. It is not used to build a profile of you across other sites. There is no advertising pixel on this domain.

Coaching and consulting engagements are separately governed by their signed agreement, including its confidentiality clause, which is stricter than this page and takes precedence for client work.

Your call

Asking what is held, or asking for it back

Write to matt@thatsgoodedesign.com and ask what is held about you, ask for a copy, ask for a correction, or ask for it to be deleted. This is a small practice, not a bureaucracy, and the answer is usually the same week.

This site is not directed at children under 13 and no information is knowingly collected from them.
