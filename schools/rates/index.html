Independent school, nonprofit and public-sector rates

Published because a business officer cannot put a line in a budget for a number nobody will tell them. The services schools buy most are listed here, and anything else is quoted. The per-participant and per-session lines are real numbers you can budget against without a call first. The cohorts and multi-day engagements depend on size and length, so those are quoted, usually within a day.
