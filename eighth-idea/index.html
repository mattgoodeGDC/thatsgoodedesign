The 8th idea

Will your school's best idea still be working in February?

A five-question check from The Leadership Lab

Experts ranked teacher coaching eighth of thirty ways to fix schools. It's the reason the top two work. Pick your lane. School leaders get five quick scenarios and a named result. Parents get five questions to ask anyone running for the school board.

    Where I can help

    I spent nearly two decades inside an independent school, and schools are one of the three lanes of my practice. The weak spots this check turns up are usually design problems, and design is the work I do with heads and senior teams:

    • Building the Bench. A year-long cohort for the teachers who could step into a coaching, chair or dean role next.
    • Delegation and role clarity. Designing a hybrid role, one section plus coaching, so the work is real, protected and owned.
    • Decision rights and governance. A working session for senior teams on who decides what, so programs stop stalling at the top.
    • Executive coaching for school leaders. One to one, for the head or senior administrator carrying the change.

    The four areas in this check come from CLUTCH, my book on building leadership systems that run without you.
