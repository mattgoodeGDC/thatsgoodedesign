Your Calendar = Your Culture

The Hidden Power of How You Spend Time

Matt Goode28 June 20251 min read Get new pieces by email

# CREATE

What you do says more than what you declare.

As a leader, your calendar is one of your most powerful cultural tools. It tells your team:

  • What do you prioritize?

  • What do you believe is worth time?

  • Who do you trust to lead in your absence?

We all say we value trust, growth, or inclusion – but do we demonstrate it in the way we spend our time? A leader’s calendar is a map of what truly matters. And here’s the key: small, intentional shifts in how you spend time can reshape your team’s culture faster than any vision statement.

The 10-Minute Calendar Audit

Set a timer. Open last week’s calendar. Ask yourself:

What did I actually prioritize?

  • What meetings or tasks did I protect, regardless of the circumstances?

  • What got bumped or delayed?

What signals did I send?

  • When I skip or reschedule something, what message does that send to my team?

  • Where did I model the values I want to build? Where didn’t I?

Where can I make one small shift this week?

  • Block time for: 1:1 connections | deep work | mentoring | reflection

  • Consider blocking “flow” time or thinking time – your coworkers don’t need to know whether you’re in a meeting or focused on strategy.

  • Trim: Meetings that drain energy & work that doesn’t align with your vision

  • And practice what you preach: model healthy habits by encouraging your team to set meeting block-out times as well.

One Coaching Prompt:


“What’s one small shift I can make this week so my time matches what I say matters?”

Try It This Week

  • Spend 10 minutes on the audit.

  • Choose one action: block a meeting, create space for thinking, delegate, or stop attending a recurring meeting that no longer serves your team.

  • Notice what changes.
