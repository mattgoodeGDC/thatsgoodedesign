The Edges Are Where the Real Work Happens

Matt Goode11 October 20252 min read Get new pieces by email

# Coaching

One Clear Idea
Big leadership moments are rare.
But micro-moments? They’re constant. And they’re shaping everything.

The hallway question.
The pause before replying.
The tone you use when someone shares an idea you didn’t expect.

These moments live at the margins - barely noticed, rarely discussed.
But over time, they become the foundation of your team’s culture and your leadership legacy.

Not the all-hands.
Not the big decision.

The edges.

A few weeks ago, I coached a manager who’d just taken over a team mid-project.

She was sharp. Clear. Vision-driven.

But morale was weird. People nodded in meetings but hesitated to speak up.

When we unpacked it, something simple emerged:

“I think I’m sending signals I don’t mean to send.”

It turned out, she was so focused on clarity and pace that she missed the micro-signals:

  • Skipping over people’s input

  • Giving fast feedback without eye contact

  • Jumping into decisions without naming what changed

None of it was dramatic. But it was all happening at the edges.
And those edges were shaping how people felt - and whether they spoke up again.

This Week’s Coaching Prompt:

“What are the smallest signals I’m sending - and what might they be saying?”

Try this reflection:

  • What happens in the 5 minutes before and after a team meeting?

  • Who do I follow up with consistently - and who do I leave hanging?

  • When someone brings me bad news, what’s my face doing before my words start?

  • What do I let slide, laugh off, or overlook that others might see as culture-setting?

These aren’t “little things.” This is leadership in disguise.

One Actionable Practice: Run a Margins Audit

Take 15 minutes this weekend. Reflect or journal:

  1. What’s one moment this week where my reaction mattered more than my plan?

  2. What tone did I set - without realizing it?

  3. What unspoken norms might I be reinforcing by accident?

  4. What’s one margin moment I could be more intentional about next week?

This isn’t about perfection. It’s about awareness.

Because leadership isn’t just built on plans. It’s built on patterns. Leadership is built on small, intentional actions and decisions.

Why This Matters Right Now

When the pace picks up, it’s easy to focus on the core:

  • The big presentation

  • The metrics

  • The agenda

But teams absorb leadership from the edges.
And the real question is:

“What am I reinforcing when I’m not trying to lead?”

That’s where trust lives.
That’s where culture hides.
That’s where influence is actually built.

Not in the script.
In the subtext.

Your Move: Tune In to the Edges

Pick one of these this week:

  • Slow down your hellos and goodbyes. These are cultural on-ramps.

  • Ask a team member, “What’s one thing I do that you think others notice - but I might not?”

  • Pause before your next big meeting and ask: “What tone do I want to carry into this room?”

Small signals. Big shift.

Because the margins aren’t peripheral.
They’re where the culture actually forms.

Please forward this to someone who’s quietly shaping culture without realizing it.

You don’t have to do more.
You have to notice what your leadership is already saying - at the edges.
