A Fresh Start for First-Time Leaders

Matt Goode10 June 20252 min read Get new pieces by email

# My role is changing

“We promoted the smartest person on the team... and now she’s drowning.”

Beginnings are hard.
Blank pages are intimidating.
But so is leading a team without a playbook.

That’s why I’m here – finally (thanks for the push, JS) – starting this Substack.

Over the past 20 years, I’ve led institutional change, coached technical and educational leaders, and developed programs such as the CREATE Leadership Framework and GoodeStart Onboarding to help first-time managers thrive. I’ve seen what happens when talented people get promoted without support, and what’s possible when they’re equipped with the right tools.

This space is for leaders who didn’t get a manual.
So we’ll build one together.

Why This, Why Now?

Because leadership development is broken.

70% of change efforts fail.
According to McKinsey, most organizational changes fail due to poor leadership and employee resistance (2023).

Burnout is up. Retention is down.
Gallup’s 2023 Global Workplace Report shows that only 23% of employees are engaged. Quiet quitting and burnout are real.

Most teams aren’t under-resourced – they’re under-led.
Despite leadership being a stated priority for 83% of organizations, only 10% feel they’re doing it well (DDI, 2023).

But leadership isn’t magic. It’s not guesswork. It’s a skill set – learnable, coachable, and repeatable.

That’s why I created Goode Design & Consulting (GDC) – to close that leadership gap with fundamental tools, tested with real teams.

And it’s why I’m launching The Leadership Lab now: to share those tools with you.

What Kind of Space Is This?

Not a newsletter. A leadership gym.

Less “thought leadership.” More useful thinking.

Here’s what you’ll find:

  • Practical systems for leading with clarity, compassion, and follow-through

  • Coaching prompts for team growth – or personal reflection

  • Field-tested tools that work when work gets messy (because it always does)

If you’re a first-time manager or a founder trying to build culture from the ground up, this is your corner of the internet.

What You’ll Get as a Reader

New posts drop Saturday mornings – just in time for your first cup of coffee – one short, clear read to start your weekend with clarity and a tool you can actually use.

Free Subscribers Get:

  • Weekly essays + coaching exercises + AI prompts

  • Signature GDC tools (like the Leadership 1:1 Template)

  • Early access to new resources and workshops

Best for: New leaders building structure, awareness, and language.

Paid Subscribers Unlock:

  • Monthly leadership case studies – real scenarios + coaching prompts

  • Previews from GoodeStart curriculum: onboarding, EQ, change leadership

  • Behind-the-scenes breakdowns of how we design coaching and strategy

  • Subscriber-only toolkits and playbooks

Why Go Paid?

Because ideas are helpful, but integration is what changes your team.

Free gives you insights.
Paid gives you reps, structure, and coaching to apply them.

Ready?

If you’ve ever said, “I wish someone had shown me how to lead…”
This is for you.

Subscribe.
Say hi in the comments.
Forward it to someone who just got promoted.

Let’s build better leaders – together.
See you next Saturday.

– Matt Goode
