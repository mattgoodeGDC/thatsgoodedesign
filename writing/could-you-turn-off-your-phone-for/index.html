Could You Turn Off Your Phone for Two Weeks Without Your Business Falling Apart?

Real leadership isn’t about being needed - it’s about building what works without you.

Matt Goode16 June 20252 min read Get new pieces by email

# Everything runs through me

Let’s be real: if you powered down your phone right now and disappeared for two weeks... would your business still be standing when you got back?

Most founders I know flinch at that question. Not because they don’t trust their team, but because they haven’t had the space to build the systems that make trust possible.

I keep thinking about a friend of mine - someone I adore. They’ve built a fantastic business. But I haven’t seen them in months.

Not because they’re flaky. Because they’re buried.

Texts hang unanswered for days. Social invites get quiet “someday” replies. Vacations? Out of the question. Even a coffee feels like asking for the moon.

I thought about reaching out when I started the CREATE Leaders program. Maybe there was alignment. But I didn’t. Not because I didn’t care, but because I realized they couldn’t even come up for air.

And that’s precisely the point.

We build these companies with sweat and smarts… and then they start to own us.

We become the glue. The funnel. The decider-of-everything. The silent shock absorber for team gaps and growing pains.

Eventually, being essential becomes exhausting.

Here’s the shift: Being the hero is unsustainable. And more than that - it’s risky.

If you being gone means everything stalls, cracks, or catches fire… then your business isn’t ready for the next chapter.

That might be growth. It might be on sale. It might just be a week off with your family, where you don’t check email at dinner.

But whatever it is, you don’t get there without replacing heroic effort with healthy systems.

A coaching prompt to try this week:

  • What’s one decision you could let someone else make?

Don’t ignore it. Just don’t own it.

Watch what happens. Reflect on how that feels. And ask yourself what it would take to do it again next week.

This is the work of authentic leadership - not just holding things together, but building something that doesn’t need you every second to function.

If that sounds like relief, let’s sit down.

Not a pitch. Just a coffee.

We can map out how to make your business feel less like a leash and more like a legacy.

– Matt

P.S. If you're wondering whether you're the friend I mentioned, you probably are. Call me. I want to go on vacation with you and BBQ on a Friday night. Let's make space for both.
