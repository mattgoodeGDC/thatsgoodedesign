It’s Not a Quiz. It’s a Mirror.

Matt Goode25 October 20253 min read Get new pieces by email

# Coaching

I Built a Tiny System That Thinks Like a Leader

A few weeks ago, I was sitting on the floor of my office – surrounded by graph paper, flowcharts, and index cards.

I wasn’t mapping a client’s org.
I was mapping mine.

Not just the work I do – but the invisible systems underneath it.
Where momentum flows.
Where friction builds.
Where I’m still the glue.

And where I needed to design better.

That session turned into a question:

“What if leadership tools weren’t just reflective – but adaptive?”

What Looks Like a Quiz, Thinks Like a System

That question became a project.
That project became a form.
But not just a form.

Here’s what’s actually under the hood:

  • 500+ lines of code

  • 115+ if/then conditionals

  • 1 form that adapts in real time

  • Custom paths based on how you lead

  • Personalized reports, written in plain language

  • Embedded coaching prompts, written by hand

  • And a fully styled UI, down to the last pixel of margin

It might look like a 90-second leadership tool.

But it’s really a systems diagnostic – for leaders who are still too central to their own system.

Why I Built It This Way

This might look like an online quiz…

But I didn’t build it to tell you who you are.
No Hogwarts house.
No “Congrats, you’re a Hufflepuff with mild burnout!”

Because you don’t need a label.
You need a mirror – one that shows where your leadership system is quietly working against you.

What I built instead is a diagnostic.

One that adapts to how you think.
One that reflects how your team is experiencing your system.
One that doesn’t flatter – but clarifies.

I wanted a tool that showed you where your structure is silently failing you.

  • When decisions continuously loop back to you

  • When momentum stalls, unless you nudge it

  • When you can’t unplug without the inbox whispering

That’s not a capacity problem.
That’s a design problem.

And good design doesn’t mean more dashboards.
It means the right questions – in the right order – that reflect your system back to you.

Try the Leadership System Diagnostic (LSD)

Yep, I called it LSD.
Because it shifts your perspective.

OK, funny enough, this acronym was totally unplanned.

Unlike some of my other more purposeful wordsmithing to get something like “CREATE” or “VOICES” – two of my actual leadership frameworks – this one was a total accident.

For most of the development, I just called it “the quiz.”But once I saw the pattern – LSD – I figured, well… it’s short, hopefully sticky, and shifts how you see things.

(Full disclosure, Mom, because she reads this – I’ve never actually done LSD…)

So LSD – – I went with it.

👉 Take it here

What you’ll get:

🧭 - Your current leadership posture
🚧 - Where friction is really coming from
🌱 - What kind of help would actually shift momentum
⏩ - What “worth it” looks like six months from now

It’s short. It’s sharp.

When I was designing it, I kept seeing a Plinko game – no two paths are ever the same. And for each answer, the quiz adapts – because your system isn’t generic.

This Week’s Coaching Prompt

Where in your system is progress still contingent on your presence – and what shift in structure or trust could change that?

Try asking:

  • What stalls when I step away?

  • What loop still depends on me to run?

  • Where is my team mirroring my uncertainty?

Your Move

✅ Take the diagnostic (Sorry, I just couldn’t call it a trip)✅ Forward it to your co-founder or team lead
✅ Use one question in your next 1:1

If a 90-second form can spot the cracks in your system…
Just imagine what a 90-day redesign could do.
