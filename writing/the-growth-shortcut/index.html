The Growth Shortcut: Why You Need a Leadership Gym

Matt Goode20 September 20252 min read Get new pieces by email

# Coaching

One Clear Idea

Leadership isn’t something you figure out in your head.

It’s something you train for – with others.

This week, I listened to Adam Galinsky on the HBR IdeaCast ("Are You an Inspirational Leader?"), and one line stuck with me:

“Leadership is like exercise – you don’t get strong by thinking about it. You get strong by doing it. Over and over.”

Galinsky’s research breaks inspirational leadership into three practices:

  • Vision: how you see the world

  • Example: how you show up in the world

  • Mentorship: how you support others in the world

All three are skills. All three can be built.
But only if you stop leading alone – and start training on purpose.

That’s where your leadership gym comes in.

This Week’s Coaching Prompt:

“Who’s helping me lead better right now?”

If your answer is “no one” or “not sure,” don’t panic.

But do pay attention.

You wouldn't train for a marathon without a plan, a community, or a coach. So why try to lead others without support?

One Actionable Tool: Build Your Leadership Gym

Take 10 minutes this weekend and run this reflection:

Who’s On Your Leadership Bench?

  • Coach – pushes you to refine your habits and decision-making

  • Cohort – walks alongside you in the same growth season

  • Mirror – reflects what you’re doing that others won’t say

  • Culture Builder – models the climate you’re trying to create

  • Anchor – holds you steady when your identity feels shaky

If you can name these people, great.

If you can’t – that’s not a failure. It’s your growth edge.

What a Trainer Gives You That You Can’t Self-Give

From Galinsky’s research and my experience working with emerging leaders, here’s what trainers (and structured support) uniquely provide:

  • Accountability that sticks – beyond “shoulds,” they keep you consistent

  • Blind spot detection – because insight rarely comes from the inside alone

  • Skill acceleration – real-time feedback > theory

  • Confidential support – safe space for hard conversations

  • Sustainable growth – reps, reflection, and rhythm over intensity

Why This Matters Right Now

The best leaders aren’t the loudest. They’re the most practiced.

They:

  • Communicate with calm and clarity

  • Model behavior they want mirrored

  • Show up when it’s uncomfortable

  • And make others feel seen, valued, and capable

None of that happens on accident.

It happens in training – especially with people who push you and believe in you.

You wouldn’t try to lift 200 lbs alone the first time.

So stop trying to carry your leadership alone either.

Your Move: Sharpen Your Bench

Take 5 minutes today:

  1. Write down the five roles: Coach, Cohort, Mirror, Culture Builder, Anchor

  2. Who fills each one for you today? Be honest.

  3. Where are the gaps?

  4. Pick one person you wish were on that list – then reach out. Ask for a conversation, not a favor.

Better yet, share this post with someone on your team and build your gyms together.

You don’t rise to the level of your ambition.

You rise to the level of your support system.

So – who’s on your bench?
