Tony Robbins Is in My Lane (And I’m Flattered)

Matt Goode30 January 20263 min read Get new pieces by email

# Off the clock

I was scrolling LinkedIn this morning when Tony Robbins appeared in my feed with a message that felt... familiar.

“BUILD THE SYSTEM THAT RUNS WITHOUT YOU.”

I checked my book cover. CLUTCH: How Leaders Build Systems That Run Without Them.

Well. Great minds and all that.

Here’s the thing: Tony’s not wrong. Well, kinda he is. The insight that being the bottleneck is a design problem, not an effort problem? That’s the central thesis of my entire book. The difference is what comes next.

Tony’s solution: Fly to San Diego. Pay... let’s just say a lot. Spend five days in a room with thousands of people while a very tall, very energized man walks you through the “7 Forces of Business Mastery.” Hope you remember the insights when you’re back at your desk on Monday, staring at the same inbox.

My solution: I show up. In your boardroom. At your leadership retreat. With your actual team, working on your actual problems.

I work with about two dozen clients right now – small business owners who built something real and got trapped inside it, technical firms whose founders can’t stop being the smartest person in every meeting, government agencies trying to build leadership capacity without the luxury of throwing money at consultants, school districts where “we’ve always done it this way” is both the diagnosis and the disease.

Here’s what changes when you fix the design: One engineering firm I worked with was hemorrhaging mid-level managers – three in six months, at roughly $65,000 a pop in recruiting and ramp-up costs. We built a simple development track. Turnover dropped to near zero.

The research supports this: clear role definition drives 20–30% higher engagement. Real delegation – not the “here’s a task, don’t screw it up” kind – cuts decision latency in half. And when trust actually runs through the system? Stephen Covey’s data suggests speed increases and costs drop by up to 50%. That’s not inspiration. That’s infrastructure.

The work is high-touch. It’s personalized. And yes, there’s a limit – I’m one person, not a stadium tour.

So if you want to be client 30 or client 50, my DMs are open. I’ll come to your off-site. I’ll facilitate your board retreat. I’ll work with your leadership team until the frameworks actually stick.

(Still waiting on my first invitation to a company retreat in Hawaii, by the way. Just putting that out there. I’m very flexible on dates.)

Tony Robbins has helped millions of people. His business empire generates $7 billion annually. He’s coached billionaires. We are not competitors in any meaningful sense.

But there’s something to be said for a coach who learns your people’s names. Who knows which of your direct reports needs a different conversation than the one you’ve been having. Who can tell you that your “delegation problem” is actually a trust problem, or a clarity problem, or a you-secretly-like-being-indispensable problem – and then help you fix it.

So yes, Tony – welcome to my lane. Kudos, your message is goode.

The execution, though? I think mine fits in your conference room.

Look, I don’t have a green “APPLY NOW” button. I don’t have an application process at all, actually. I have coffee. (Or video chat, if you’re not in the Baltimore/DC area and refuse to fly me somewhere interesting.)

Here’s how this works: You reach out. We talk. I learn what’s actually going on – not the investor pitch version, the real version. If I can help, I’ll tell you how. If I can’t, I’ll tell you that, too. No one’s getting upsold on a platinum mastermind tier.

DMs are open; find me on LinkedIn. Email works too: matt@thatsgoodedesign.com. Or grab CLUTCH when it drops next month – it’s everything I know about building systems that don’t require you to be the smartest, hardest-working person in every room. (You can still be that person. You just won’t have to be.)

And if you want more like this – frameworks, hard-won lessons from twenty years of watching leaders get in their own way – subscribe to this Substack. It's free. It lands in your inbox. It won't yell at you in all caps about UNLOCKING YOUR POTENTIAL.

And, if your company happens to hold retreats in Hawaii, I remain available.

Extremely available.

Suspiciously available.
