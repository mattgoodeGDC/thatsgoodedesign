The Patrol Check

Are you developing your leaders, or hosting them?

From the Leadership Lab essay "Don’t Hire the Eagle Scout. Build the Patrol." Read it here →

A Scout patrol grows leaders because of how it is built, not because of the badge. Four parts do the work: a real job, real authority, no rescue and regular reps. Think of the people you are trying to grow into leaders, and answer four questions about each of them. A no on any one means you aren’t developing that person yet. You’re hosting them.

About two minutes. First names or initials are fine. Everything stays in this browser; nothing is sent or saved.

    Where I can help

    A no on this check is a design problem, not a people problem, and design is the work I do with founders and school leaders.

    • GoodeStart. The first ninety days of a new leader, built so the job, the authority and the reps are real from day one.
    • Building the Bench. A cohort for the people who could step into the next seat, with real work between sessions.
    • Executive coaching. For the manager who has to keep their hands in their pockets.

    The system behind the patrol is in CLUTCH, my book on building leadership that runs without you.
